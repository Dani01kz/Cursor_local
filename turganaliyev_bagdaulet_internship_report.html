Introduction

From April 13 to May 16, 2026, I completed my pre-graduation internship at TOO "WORLD GLOBAL TRADE CO" as a system administrator intern. During the internship, I gained practical experience in maintaining office IT infrastructure, preparing workstations, supporting users, configuring network access, and solving everyday technical problems that occur in a business environment.

The main purpose of the internship was to apply the knowledge gained during my studies to real tasks related to computer systems, operating systems, local networks, office equipment, and user support. I worked under the guidance of experienced employees and gradually became familiar with the daily responsibilities of a system administrator.

During the practice, I participated in installing and configuring Windows operating systems, downloading and installing drivers, setting up office software, checking Internet access, connecting printers, diagnosing hardware and software problems, and assisting employees with technical requests. These tasks helped me better understand how important stable IT infrastructure is for the normal operation of a company.

This report describes the main activities I performed during the internship, the tools and technologies I used, and the professional skills I developed. The report is divided into four sections: familiarization with the company IT environment, workstation preparation and Windows configuration, network and office equipment support, and technical support and system administration practice.

Paragraph 1. Familiarization with the Company IT Environment.

At the beginning of my internship at TOO "WORLD GLOBAL TRADE CO", I became familiar with the company's internal work process, workplace discipline, safety rules, and the responsibilities of the IT support staff. I learned how the company uses computers, office software, printers, network access, and Internet services in daily business operations.

I studied the basic structure of the office IT environment, including user workstations, network equipment, printers, Wi-Fi access, shared folders, and the main software used by employees. I observed how system administrators organize technical support, respond to user requests, and keep equipment ready for stable work.

During this stage, I learned that the work of a system administrator requires accuracy and responsibility, because even small technical problems can interrupt the work of employees. For this reason, it is important to check equipment carefully, document completed actions, and solve issues in a way that prevents repeated failures.

I also became familiar with the common tools used for diagnostics and configuration, such as Windows Settings, Device Manager, Control Panel, command-line utilities, antivirus tools, browser settings, printer management tools, and basic network checking commands. This gave me a foundation for the practical tasks I completed later during the internship.

Paragraph 2. Workstation Preparation, Windows Installation, and Driver Setup.

A major part of my internship was related to preparing computers for employees. I installed Windows operating systems from scratch on computers that needed full reinstallation or initial configuration. Before installation, I checked the condition of the device, prepared the bootable installation media, selected the required disk partition, and followed the standard Windows installation process.

After installing Windows, I downloaded and installed the necessary drivers for stable operation of the computer. These included chipset, video, audio, network adapter, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and printer drivers. I used Device Manager to check whether all hardware components were recognized correctly and whether any unknown devices remained in the system.

I also configured the operating system for daily work. This included setting the language and region, installing updates, creating or preparing user accounts, configuring Internet access, installing office applications, browsers, archive tools, PDF readers, and other programs required for work. I checked that each computer could connect to the network and perform basic tasks without errors.

This part of the internship improved my practical skills in system administration. I learned that a correctly prepared workstation must be stable, updated, secure, and convenient for the user. I also understood the importance of checking every driver and program after installation, because incomplete configuration can create problems for employees later.

Paragraph 3. Network Access and Office Equipment Support.

During the internship, I also participated in tasks related to network access and office equipment. I checked Internet connectivity on workstations, verified IP configuration, tested Wi-Fi access, and helped identify the reasons for connection problems. When a computer could not access the Internet, I checked the cable connection, network adapter status, router or switch indicators, and Windows network settings.

I practiced basic network troubleshooting using tools such as ping, ipconfig, and network adapter settings. These tools helped me determine whether the problem was related to the computer, network configuration, cable connection, or Internet access. I also learned how to restart network devices correctly and how to check whether the connection was restored after troubleshooting.

Another part of my work was connected with office equipment. I helped connect and configure printers, install printer drivers, set default printers, and check printing from user computers. When printing problems occurred, I checked the printer connection, paper and toner status, print queue, driver installation, and network availability of the printer.

These tasks helped me understand that system administration includes not only computers, but also the whole office IT environment. Stable network access and correctly configured office equipment are necessary for employees to complete their daily work efficiently.

Paragraph 4. Technical Support and System Administration Practice.

In the final stage of the internship, I focused on user support and general system administration tasks. I assisted employees with common technical issues, including slow computer performance, missing drivers, software errors, Internet connection problems, printer issues, browser problems, and basic Windows configuration requests.

For each request, I tried to identify the problem step by step. I asked the user what happened, checked the visible symptoms, reviewed system settings, tested the affected device, and then applied the necessary solution. In some cases, the problem was solved by updating drivers, reinstalling software, clearing unnecessary startup programs, restarting services, or correcting network settings.

I also learned the importance of communication in technical support. Users do not always know the exact technical reason for a problem, so it is necessary to explain actions in simple words, remain calm, and make sure that the user can continue working after the issue is resolved. This helped me develop not only technical skills, but also professional communication skills.

Technical support practice showed me that the role of a system administrator is important for business continuity. A system administrator must be able to respond quickly, analyze problems carefully, maintain equipment, protect systems, and support users in their daily work.

Conclusion

The pre-graduation internship at TOO "WORLD GLOBAL TRADE CO" was a valuable practical experience for me. During the internship, I became familiar with the real work of a system administrator and learned how IT infrastructure supports the daily activities of a company.

I gained hands-on experience in installing Windows, downloading and installing drivers, configuring computers for work, checking network access, connecting printers, installing software, and solving common technical problems. These tasks helped me strengthen my knowledge of operating systems, hardware, networks, and user support.

The internship also showed me the importance of responsibility, accuracy, and documentation in system administration. Every computer, network connection, printer, and software configuration must be checked carefully so that employees can work without interruptions.

I would like to express my gratitude to the employees and supervisors of TOO "WORLD GLOBAL TRADE CO" for their guidance and support during my internship. The practical knowledge and skills I gained during this period will be useful for my future professional development in system administration, technical support, and information systems.
