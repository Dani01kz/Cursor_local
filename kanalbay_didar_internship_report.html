Introduction

From April 13 to May 16, 2026, I completed my pre-graduation internship at JSC "Kazakhtelecom" in the Retail Business Division. During the internship, I was able to gain practical experience in the field of telecommunications, customer connection services, computer preparation, and technical support. This practice helped me understand how theoretical knowledge from my studies is applied in a real working environment.

At the beginning of the internship, I worked with specialists who install Internet services for customers. I observed and participated in the preparation of equipment, connection of subscriber devices, configuration of routers, and basic checking of Internet availability after installation. This stage gave me an understanding of how Internet access is delivered to users and what steps are required to make the service stable and convenient for the customer.

In the later part of the internship, I completed practice in technical support. My tasks were connected with helping users solve common technical problems, checking network settings, preparing computers for work, installing Windows from scratch, downloading and installing drivers, and configuring software needed for daily work. These tasks improved my practical skills in system administration and user support.

The main purpose of this report is to describe the activities I performed during the internship, the tools and technologies I used, the challenges I faced, and the professional skills I developed. The report is organized into four main sections: introduction to the organization and subscriber services, Internet installation and equipment configuration, workstation preparation and Windows setup, and technical support activities.

Paragraph 1. Organization Familiarization and Retail Business Services.

During the initial stage of the internship at JSC "Kazakhtelecom", I became familiar with the work of the Retail Business Division and the general structure of customer service processes. I was introduced to the rules of workplace discipline, safety requirements, internal communication standards, and the main responsibilities of employees who work with individual subscribers.

I learned that the Retail Business Division plays an important role in providing Internet, telephone, and digital services to private customers. The division works directly with subscribers and is responsible for connecting new services, maintaining existing services, receiving customer requests, and ensuring that technical problems are solved as quickly as possible.

At the beginning of the practice, I worked with installation specialists. I studied how customer requests are received, how technical tasks are assigned, and how specialists prepare the required equipment before visiting subscribers. I became familiar with routers, network cables, optical network terminals, connectors, and other tools used during Internet installation and maintenance.

I also observed how specialists communicate with customers, explain the installation process, check the availability of service, and provide basic recommendations for using Wi-Fi and Internet equipment. This helped me understand that successful work in telecommunications requires not only technical knowledge, but also accuracy, responsibility, and clear communication with users.

Paragraph 2. Internet Installation and Network Equipment Configuration.

A significant part of my internship was connected with practical work related to Internet installation. Together with experienced specialists, I participated in preparing subscriber equipment and checking network connections. I learned how Internet access is provided to customers and what steps must be completed before the service can be considered ready for use.

During installation tasks, I helped prepare routers and connect them to the subscriber line. I checked cable connections, power indicators, Internet status indicators, and Wi-Fi availability. I also studied how basic router settings are configured, including wireless network name, password, security mode, and connection parameters required for correct access to the provider network.

After installation, I participated in testing the connection. This included checking whether the computer or mobile device received an IP address, opening web pages, measuring Internet speed, and confirming that the Wi-Fi network worked in different parts of the room. If the connection was unstable, I helped check the cable, restart the router, verify settings, and identify possible causes of the problem.

This stage of the internship helped me improve my understanding of local networks, subscriber equipment, Wi-Fi configuration, and basic troubleshooting. I also learned that even small mistakes, such as an incorrectly connected cable or an incorrectly entered password, can prevent the customer from using the service normally.

Paragraph 3. Workstation Preparation, Windows Installation, and Driver Setup.

Another important part of my internship was connected with preparing computers for work. I installed Windows operating systems from scratch on workstations that required full reinstallation or preparation for further use. Before installation, I checked the basic condition of the computer, selected the required boot device, prepared the installation media, and followed the standard installation steps.

After installing Windows, I downloaded and installed the necessary drivers for stable operation of the computer. These included drivers for the chipset, network adapter, video adapter, audio device, and other hardware components. I checked Device Manager to make sure that the system recognized all devices correctly and that there were no unknown devices or driver errors.

I also configured the operating system for daily work. This included installing updates, setting system language and regional options, configuring network access, installing required applications, connecting printers when needed, and checking that the computer could access the Internet and local resources. In some cases, I also installed basic security tools and checked that the system was ready for safe use.

This work gave me practical experience in computer maintenance and system administration. I learned how important it is to install the correct drivers, keep the system updated, and check every component after installation. I also improved my ability to diagnose common computer problems and prepare a workstation so that an employee can immediately start working without additional technical difficulties.

Paragraph 4. Technical Support and User Assistance.

In the final stage of the internship, I practiced in technical support. This part of the practice allowed me to work with user requests and understand how support specialists identify and solve technical problems. I observed how requests are received, clarified, prioritized, and transferred to the responsible specialists when necessary.

I assisted users with common Internet and computer problems. These included loss of Internet connection, weak Wi-Fi signal, incorrect network settings, router restart issues, slow computer performance, missing drivers, and software configuration problems. For each request, I tried to determine the cause step by step: checking physical connections, network status, IP settings, router indicators, and the condition of the operating system.

When users had computer-related problems, I helped check Windows settings, update drivers, reinstall required programs, and test whether the issue was resolved. I also learned how to communicate with users in a calm and clear way, because many customers or employees may not know technical terms. It was important to explain actions simply and make sure that the user understood what had been done.

Technical support practice showed me that troubleshooting is not only about knowing commands or programs. It also requires patience, careful analysis, documentation of completed actions, and the ability to ask the right questions. This experience was very useful for developing professional skills in IT support and telecommunications service maintenance.

Conclusion

The pre-graduation internship at JSC "Kazakhtelecom" in the Retail Business Division was a valuable practical experience for me. During the internship, I became familiar with the work of a major telecommunications company and learned how customer Internet services are installed, configured, maintained, and supported.

At the beginning of the practice, I gained experience in Internet installation and subscriber equipment configuration. I learned how routers and network devices are prepared, how connections are checked, and how specialists confirm that the customer can use the service successfully. This helped me better understand the practical side of computer networks and telecommunications.

During the internship, I also worked with computers and operating systems. I installed Windows from scratch, downloaded and installed drivers, configured systems for work, checked hardware recognition, and prepared computers for users. These tasks strengthened my practical skills in system administration and computer maintenance.

In the final part of the practice, I worked in technical support, where I learned how to communicate with users, identify technical problems, and solve common issues related to Internet access, routers, Windows settings, drivers, and software. This experience taught me the importance of responsibility, attention to detail, and clear communication.

I would like to express my gratitude to the employees and supervisors of JSC "Kazakhtelecom" for their guidance and support during the internship. The knowledge and skills I gained during this practice will be useful for my future professional development in information systems, computer networks, and technical support.
